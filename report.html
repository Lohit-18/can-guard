Detection by attack family

Two families are held out of training entirely and are the zero-day test. The supervised models score near zero on them; the anomaly detector does not, which is the whole argument for running both.

Detection by threat model

Injection is loud - the message rate doubles and the counters break. Masquerade is not: the attacker silenced the real ECU and transmits in its place with correct timing, counters and checksums. Adaptive goes further and imitates the victim's clock skew too.
